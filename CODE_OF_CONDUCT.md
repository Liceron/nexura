# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to contribute.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive; disagree with evidence.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Do not share others' private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and restrict participation, for
behavior that breaks these expectations.

Reporting: email info@liceron.in. Reports are handled confidentially by the
maintainers at Liceron.
